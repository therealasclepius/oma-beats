Choose a kit

Your beat starts here.

Hit the pads or use the matching keyboard keys. Click the small squares to build a pattern, then press Space to play.

Record: turn on Record and start playback. Your pad hits use the timing strength you choose. Name and duplicate sequences, choose up to 16 bars, and arrange them in Song. Use Performance & MIDI for count-in, note repeat, triplets, recording mode and timing strength.

Samples: select a pad and load or drop an audio file. Adjust its start, end, pitch, and level. The Chop editor finds transients, splits evenly, or lets you place and drag markers. Choose where to assign your chops. Mono chops cuts off the preceding sound. Keyboard → pads spreads one sound across 16 semitone pitches.

Save: your session saves locally, including samples. Save project creates a portable backup including your loaded samples. Export WAV renders the selected sequence or song with mixer effects and tails. Each mixer track can export its own stem. Backups keeps five recent local recovery points.

Built-in sounds are synthesized locally. No account or internet needed.
