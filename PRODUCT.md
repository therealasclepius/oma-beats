# Oma Beats

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Beatmakers who want a hands-on desktop sampler, drum sequencer, sample chopping, and simple melodic instruments. The initial user works on Omarchy Linux and uses local samples and licensed Splice packs.

## Product Purpose

Build beats with performance pads, chopped samples, and built-in sounds; save portable projects and export WAV audio.

## Operating Context

A standalone Electron desktop app with an HTML/CSS/Web Audio interface. Public source and downloadable preview builds live at github.com/therealasclepius/oma-beats. Linux x86_64, Windows x64 and Apple Silicon macOS builds exist.

## Capabilities and Constraints

128 pads in eight banks, four 16-step patterns, per-bank mono playback, sample workshop, undo/redo, local pack import, six synthesized drum kits, and 36 synth presets with editable envelopes and filters. Synth notes are rendered samples. Optional YouTube importing requires external yt-dlp and FFmpeg. No VST hosting, MIDI input, song arrangement, or independent time stretching. Builds are unsigned previews. Third-party packs and personal projects are not bundled or published. Public source is UNLICENSED, not open source.

## Brand Commitments

Oma Beats name and existing cream, sage green, and orange identity. User requested an original high-quality webpage using Impeccable and their Distinctive AI Design skill. The previous Omarchy Sequencer reference is withdrawn. Include an Omarchy install link/command.

## Evidence on Hand

Working desktop application under app/ and desktop/, automated tests, native smoke checks and successful platform builds. No customer counts, testimonials, commercial endorsements, or public community song library have been supplied. Website demo compositions may be original and clearly labeled.

## Product Principles

- Make sounds and controls immediately accessible.
- Preserve projects and keep editing reversible.
- Keep personal samples local.
- Let the desktop app work offline without an account.
